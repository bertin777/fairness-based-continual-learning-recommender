========================================================================
FAIRNESS-AWARE CONTINUAL LEARNING RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM DATASET
========================================================================

Contents:
1. users.csv (100 rows): Customer demographic groups, activity tiers, and preferred categories.
   - Group A: Standard Shoppers (50%)
   - Group B: Premium Shoppers (50%)

2. products_catalog.csv (40 rows): E-commerce items across 5 domains (Electronics, Fashion, Home, Beauty, Books).

3. interactions_batches.csv (1,260 rows): Chronological user interactions partitioned across 6 retraining rounds.
   - Demonstrates feedback loops and fairness drift from Round 1 to Round 6.

Generated for Phase 1 prototype of 'Fairness-Aware Continual Learning: Building Recommendation Systems for E-commerce Websites That Adapt Without Losing Fairness'.
